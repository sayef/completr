# Code of Conduct

completr follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short, we want completr to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not
  acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at [msi.sayef@gmail.com](mailto:msi.sayef@gmail.com). All reports are reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may ban
contributors, when this code is not followed. The full enforcement guidelines are in the Contributor
Covenant.
